Signature Feature

The Canon System.

Canon & Echoes

Every story world has two layers. Canon is the official story — the timeline, characters, and events the creator endorses. Echoes are community expansions: fan stories, alternate timelines, and "what if?" scenarios that branch from the canon. A creator can promote an Echo into official canon — and when they do, the fan who wrote it is permanently credited as a contributor. The Canon System keeps a story's official spine intact while letting the community imagine everything around it.
