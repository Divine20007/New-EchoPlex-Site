FAQ

Common questions.

Is EchoPlex a crypto project?

No. EchoPlex is a digital entertainment ecosystem. Blockchain is infrastructure, not identity. Users may enjoy the platform for months before ever connecting a wallet.

When will EchoPlex launch?

EchoPlex follows a phased roadmap. Phase 1 focuses on foundation — identity, profiles, and community building. Development timelines are strategic objectives, not guaranteed dates.

Do I need a wallet to join?

No. Registration uses familiar methods like email or Google. A cryptocurrency wallet is entirely optional.

Will EchoPlex be free to use?

Most core experiences will be free. Optional premium features, marketplace transactions, and future ecosystem services may introduce paid functionality without restricting access to the platform itself.

How can creators earn?

Creators can earn through marketplace sales, tips, commissions, subscriptions, licensing, and collaborative revenue sharing. ECP rewards recognize all forms of contribution.
