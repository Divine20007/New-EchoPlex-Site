Two Layers of Belonging

Open spaces and structured teams.

CM

Communities

Open spaces around shared interests — game communities, programming, cybersecurity, AI, digital art, music production, anime, fantasy writing, blockchain, education, entrepreneurship, and photography. Anyone can join; everyone can participate.

GU

Guilds

Structured groups with shared goals — competitive esports teams, creative studios, developer teams, writing groups, art collectives, university clubs, mentorship organizations, and innovation labs. Guilds are where casual interest becomes committed collaboration.

Guild Foundations

Leadership earned, not bought.

GS

Guild Structure

Roles — Founder, Leader, Officer, Moderator, Mentor, Recruiter, Event Organizer, Member. Leadership is earned through trust and contribution, not purchased or inherited. Every role carries real responsibilities the guild can see.

GP

Guild Profiles

Description, mission, founding date, leadership, member count, achievements, events, published projects, and guild level. A guild's profile is its public résumé — and its members' shared identity.

Growth & Activity

Guilds level up by doing things together.

GP

Guild Progression

Hosting events, winning competitions, publishing projects, helping newcomers, running workshops, and creating collaborative work all advance a guild's level. Progression reflects real contribution — not just time served.

EV

Events

Game tournaments, hackathons, writing contests, art exhibitions, music showcases, workshops, AMAs, community game nights, and charity fundraisers. A continuous calendar of reasons to show up.

People & Safety

Lift others up. Keep the space safe.

ME

Mentorship

Guide beginners, review projects, teach workshops, answer questions, and recommend learning resources. Mentorship is recognized as a first-class contribution to the ecosystem.

CR

Community Recognition

Community Builder, Helpful Mentor, Outstanding Moderator, Event Organizer, Top Contributor, Guild Founder, Creative Leader, and Educator. Recognition titles reflect real service — and show on your identity.

SM

Safety & Moderation

Clear guidelines, transparent moderation, reporting systems, appeal processes, and anti-harassment policies. Safety is built into the structure — not bolted on after a problem.

Signature Feature

Guild Headquarters.

A customizable digital home for every guild

Every guild gets a customizable digital headquarters — a single space that holds everything the guild is and does. It includes a Guild Hall, Announcement Board, Event Calendar, Trophy Room, Member Directory, Knowledge Library, Showcase Gallery, Active Games, Stories Created, a Marketplace Collection, and Guild Statistics. A Guild HQ isn't just a chat channel — it's the public face and private workshop of a committed group, all in one place.
